# DuoFrost 2.5.0

The full release for LED control on AYN devices combines the refreshed interface,
Smart Scenes and the new preset library tools. It builds on the 2.3.0 interface
and the 2.5.0 alphas, with final visual and reliability improvements.

**[Download the signed APK](https://github.com/Tufein/DuoFrost/releases/download/v2.5.0/DuoFrost-2.5.0.apk)** ·
[Release notes, source and checksums](https://github.com/Tufein/DuoFrost/releases/tag/v2.5.0).

## Find your lighting quickly

The dashboard brings lighting status, the selected preset, output limit, mute and
sleep timer together. **Edit lighting** opens the lighting controls; **Smart
Scenes** opens automation. Settings are grouped into Lighting, Device, Appearance
and Wallpaper. Narrow windows stack the dashboard and library; wide windows place
them side by side, including on short landscape screens.

Searching, filtering and organising presets leave your lighting unchanged. Tap a
preset card to apply it when manual switching is active. Open its **⋮** menu to
preview, favourite, organise or edit it.

## Let Smart Scenes choose

1. Save a lighting preset.
2. Open **Smart Scenes** from the dashboard or Device settings.
3. Add a scene and choose a preset, or choose **only limit brightness**.
4. Choose apps or games, hours, weekdays, battery level or power source.
5. Enable Smart Scenes and start lighting normally.

All conditions in a scene must match. Exact app profiles take precedence over
general scene rules. Among matching scenes, higher priority wins; equal priorities
follow the displayed rule order. Brightness-only rules combine using the lowest
limit. Your overall brightness limit and mute still apply.

For example, create **Night dimming** with a 20% brightness limit from 22:00 until
07:00. It dims your current preset instead of replacing it. Overnight weekday
rules follow the day on which the period starts.

Create app groups for emulators, favourite games or media apps when Android's
game recognition is incomplete. Game and app-group rules need Usage Access;
time and battery rules work without it. Matching happens at the Android app
level, rather than at the level of individual ROMs inside an emulator.

Hold a temporary preset for **15, 30, 60 or 120 minutes**, or use **Resume** to
return to automation early. When a hold ends, DuoFrost checks the current rules
again. Stop and reboot clear the hold. Editing or enabling rules leaves stopped
lighting off. Capture effects can ask for permission when a new session is needed.

## Organise and preview presets

Favourites appear first. Use search with **All presets**, **Favourites** or a named
collection to narrow the library. **Collections** lets you create, rename and
remove collections; presets can belong to several collections. Removing a
collection keeps its presets.

Large libraries initially show 40 matching presets. **Show more presets** loads
another 40.

Choose **Preview effect** from a preset's menu to see its colours and brightness
on two illustrated rings. Supported animations can be played or paused, and stop
when the preview leaves the foreground. Previews respect Android's disabled
animations setting.

The rings illustrate the look. Ambient, audio, sensor-driven, random and flashing
effects use still examples with an explanation. Previewing needs no capture
permission and leaves saved settings and physical LEDs unchanged. Check the final
look on your device.

## Review before importing

Open a preset bundle through the existing import control, then:

1. Select the presets you want.
2. Choose **Add separate copies**, **Replace matching user presets**, or
   **Skip matching names**.
3. Optionally enable **Also import app assignments for selected presets**.
4. Check the added/replaced/skipped summary and save.

Separate copies are the default and receive unique names. Replacing a matching
user preset keeps its identity and scene links. Presets managed by apps or plugins
are protected; replacement mode adds a separate copy instead.

App assignments are off by default. When enabled, selected imported assignments
merge with your existing assignments, adjusting copied preset names. An assignment
for the same app can change. Cancel leaves the saved library and assignments
unchanged. Saving leaves stopped lighting off; running automation can use updated
presets or assignments.

## Undo and back up

After an eligible import or deletion, **Undo** appears for ten minutes. It restores
the saved presets, favourites, collection memberships and app assignments from
before that action, retaining any artwork needed for recovery.

One undo is available at a time. A newer eligible action replaces it, and later
relevant library or assignment edits make it unavailable. Very large changes can
exceed the recovery limit; the app tells you when undo cannot be offered.

Full backups with **Profiles** selected include scene rules, app groups,
favourites and collections. Temporary holds, temporary undo and Android permission
grants are excluded. Restoring profiles or images clears pending undo.

## Install and update

Back up before updating and keep that backup outside the app. The signed APK
updates production **2.1.0, 2.3.0 and both 2.5.0 alphas** in place using the same
release key, preserving presets and settings.

Original Version 1 and Version 2 need the
[one-time backup and reinstall procedure](../README.md). DuoFrost can import
BiFrost presets, themes and backups, and installs alongside BiFrost. Run one LED
controller at a time.

**Hidden feature — tidy Recent apps:** DuoFrost's window stays out of the Recent
apps list. Reopen it from its icon; use Start/Stop to control lighting.
